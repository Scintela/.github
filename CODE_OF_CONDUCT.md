# 行为准则 / Code of Conduct

## 中文

Scintela 采用 [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) 的精神作为行为准则。核心承诺:

- 保持友善与耐心,尊重不同背景、不同水平的参与者——这里既有资深工程师,也有第一次点灯的学生
- 不接受骚扰、人身攻击、歧视性或其他敌意言论
- 以社区整体利益为先,接受对作品的善意批评
- 注重建设性:提出问题时尽量附带可操作的改进建议

违反行为准则的行为,请举报至 **security@scintela.dev**,或联系任一组织管理员。所有举报将被认真对待并保密处理;查实后视情节给予警告直至移出社区。

## English

Scintela follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

- Be friendly and patient. Respect participants of all backgrounds and skill levels — from veteran engineers to students lighting their first LED.
- Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination and other hostile conduct are not accepted.
- Prioritize the community as a whole and accept constructive criticism of your work.
- Be constructive: when raising a problem, bring an actionable suggestion if you can.

Please report violations to **security@scintela.dev** or any organization admin. All reports are taken seriously and handled confidentially; confirmed violations may result in a warning up to removal from the community.
